Executive Judging & Scientific Verification Brief

SPHEREx // CHRONOS is an interactive time-domain infrared discovery platform built on live NASA/IPAC IRSA SIAv2 open data (spherex_dr1 & spherex_qr3). It combines multi-epoch astrometric blinking, split-curtain comparison, dipole difference imaging, and on-the-fly 102-channel spectroscopy (0.75–5.00 μm).

1. Validity & Astrometric Epoch Propagation

Propagates J2000/2010 reference coordinates to the 2025–2026 SPHEREx observation epoch via 3D space motion (e.g. Barnard's Star +266.5" N, WISE 0855 −121.5" W), reprojects raw FITS cutouts onto North-Up WCS grids, and anchors 102-band spectra to real 2MASS + WISE mJy fluxes.

2. Live Space Agency Data (IRSA SIAv2 + Gator + IBE)

Queries live NASA/IPAC IRSA SIAv2 exposures, live IRSA Gator catalogs (gaia_dr3_source & allwise_p3as_psd), 7-HDU Level-2 FITS cutouts, 32-bit HDU 2 FLAGS bitmasks, and CSA NEOSSat (CADC) cross-links.

3. Data Provenance & Forward-Modeling Methodology

All sky coordinates, observation timestamps (MJD), spacecraft WCS footprints, and native detector cutouts (RAW_FITS) are fetched from official NASA/IPAC IRSA (SIAv2, IBE, and Gator gaia_dr3_source + allwise_p3as_psd) archives. Because individual SPHEREx Level-2 frames sample a single LVF strip per pass, MODEL_CUBE reconstructs continuous 6-band + 102-channel spectrophotometry anchored to cataloged 2MASS/WISE flux densities (mJy); switch to RAW_FITS anytime to inspect un-modeled North-Up WCS NASA detector cutouts.
